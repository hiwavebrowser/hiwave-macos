fix(svg,engine,renderer): currentColor reaches inline SVG; :hover/:focus-within on an ancestor no longer match; stroked circles are rings

Three defects, the last two exposed by the first colored pixels the shelf's
search icon ever painted (pinned Chrome 148 strokes it rgb(148,163,184);
we stroked it black, then in the accent color with a white disc inside).

1. currentColor. rustkit-svg resolved Paint::CurrentColor to BLACK ("would
   need context"). The CSS color is now a render-time input:
   SvgStyle.current_color (inherited unconditionally), fill_color()/
   stroke_color() resolve through Paint::resolve, and
   SvgDocument::render_with_color carries it; render() keeps black for the
   standalone <img src=*.svg> document. DisplayCommand::Image gains
   current_color, filled from the box's computed color in
   render_replaced_content and handed to the engine's svg splice.

2. Pseudo-classes on an ancestor compound were never evaluated.
   simple_selector_matches_ancestor parsed tag/class/id and broke at the
   first ':' — so `.card:hover .title` and `.wrapper:focus-within .icon`
   matched with the state off on every page. And match_pseudo_class's
   static-false list lacked focus-within/focus-visible/target, which fell
   to its `_ => true` arm. One pseudo_class_is_static_false() now serves
   both matchers. T-RED: the new engine test fails on the subject-only fix
   with the board's own number (left: (34, 211, 238)).

3. StrokeCircle painted a colored disc and then an opaque WHITE disc of
   radius r-w over it. Every fill="none" circle icon on a dark surface
   carried a white disc, and the stroke sat inside the geometry instead of
   centred on it (SVG 2 §13.4, r ± w/2). Replaced with draw_ring, a
   triangle strip between the two radii.

Tests: rustkit-svg +1 (currentColor resolves to the render call's color;
render() stays black), rustkit-layout images +1 (Image command carries the
box color), rustkit-engine +1 (:focus-within / :focus-visible / ancestor
:hover do not match in the static frame). Suites: svg 16, layout 369,
renderer 64, engine 82.
