fix(layout): a push button has Chrome's default box and its own line

Chrome's UA sheet gives a button `padding: 1px 6px`, a 2px border,
`box-sizing: border-box` and `line-height: normal`. The engine's default
style gave it none of these, and layout stood in a fixed figure for a
style with no padding or border: label + 24 wide, 19 tall. So a bare
"Go" was 41.79 x 19 where Chrome 148 builds 33.80 x 21, a `padding: 0;
border: 0` reset was still label + 24 (Chrome: the label alone, 17.80 x
15), and `line-height` never reached the height (`line-height: 30px`:
19 here, 36 in Chrome).

- engine: `<button>` and `<input type=button|submit|reset>` get the
  default padding, border and box-sizing in the UA arm, and do not take
  the page's line-height unless the author says `inherit`.
- layout: a button's intrinsic size is always its label and line inside
  its padding and border; a button with no label has no line (16 x 6).
- layout: the part of a button under its label's baseline counts its
  bottom padding and border, so buttons of different padding on one
  line share a baseline. They shared a bottom edge.

Text fields and selects keep their calibrated sizes; they are next.
