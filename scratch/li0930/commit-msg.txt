fix(css,engine): cascade layers rank rules before specificity

`@layer` blocks were flattened into plain rules in source order, so layer
precedence (CSS Cascade 5 §6.4) was ignored. linkedin now A/B-serves a
1.3 MB sheet that is all layers (reset, theme, rootTheme, derivedTheme,
localization, atoms, overrides). Flattened, the reset layer's rules beat
the rules that style the page by specificity, and the page rendered close
to unstyled.

- rustkit-cssparser: each rule carries its full dotted layer name (nested
  blocks accumulate; each anonymous block gets a unique name), and the
  sheet records every `@layer a, b;` statement and block opening with its
  position and enclosing `@media`.
- rustkit-css: `assign_layer_order` ranks layers across the document's
  sheets by first declaration, sublayers before their parent's own rules,
  and unlayered rules above all (`UNLAYERED`).
- rustkit-engine: the build ranks layers after the `@media` filter
  (statements in a non-matching `@media` are dropped, positions re-counted).
  The cascade sorts by (layer, specificity, source order), and the
  `!important` pass takes layers in reverse, for elements and for
  `::before`/`::after`. A page with no `@layer` keeps the old order.

Pins: 6 engine tests through `build_layout_from_document` (all fail with
the ranking disabled), a selector sanity pin so they cannot pass
vacuously, a rustkit-css ranking test and 2 parser tests.
