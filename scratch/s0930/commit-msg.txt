fix(engine): keep an empty block in the layout tree when it still affects layout

Box construction dropped every childless block without "visible styling"
(size, background, border, padding). Some empty blocks still change layout:

- an empty formatting root (flex, grid, overflow) never collapses through,
  so body's 8px and the next block's 8px stack to 16 around it; dropped,
  they collapsed to 8;
- an empty block's vertical margins collapse through into the flow
  (`<div style="margin-top:30px"></div>` moves what follows);
- min-height and clear give an empty box extent or clearance;
- an empty flex/grid item takes a slot, a gap and free space (a `flex:1`
  spacer came out 0 wide, pulling its siblings left).

All four pins match pinned Chrome 148 (scratch probe) and fail without the
fix. Margin pins go through the engine page path only: `layout()` doesn't
collapse sibling margins at all, before or after this change.
