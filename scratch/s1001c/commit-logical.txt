fix(engine): flow-relative border properties map to the physical sides

`border-inline-*` and `border-block-*` had no arms, so a declaration
written with them was dropped. StyleX and Tailwind v4 emit them:
facebook's login inputs painted a top and a bottom border and no left or
right one.

`logical_to_physical` gains the per-side shorthands and longhands
(`border-inline-start`, `border-block-end-width`, ...), the two-value
`border-inline-width|style|color` and `border-block-*` forms, and
`border-inline` / `border-block`, whose whole value goes to both sides.
A two-value logical shorthand is now split at top-level whitespace, so
`rgb(1, 2, 3)` is one value (this also fixes `margin-inline:
calc(1px + 2px)`, which was dropped as three values).

Horizontal-tb, ltr only, as for the margin, padding and inset names.
