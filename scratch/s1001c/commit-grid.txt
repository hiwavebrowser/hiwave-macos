fix(renderer): lay a corner's pixel grid from the box's outer edge

A right or bottom corner's cells were laid from the corner box's inner
corner. With a whole-pixel radius that is the same grid as the outer
edge's. With a fractional one (`25%` of 150px is 37.5px) it sits half a
pixel off: the pixels along the outer edge were measured on the curve
instead of inside it and painted half transparent, a notch in the side
where the corner ends.

`corner_cells` lays the grid from the outer edge for all four corners
and cuts the cell that crosses the box's inner end, so that pixel is
painted once (the old loop let it stick out under the strip beside it).
The fill and the border share it. A whole-pixel box gets the same cells
as before.
