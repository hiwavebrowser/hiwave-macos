fix(text): a face without a family name keeps its own ascent instead of panicking

`blink_ascent` read `CTFont::family_name`, which panics when Core Text has
no family name for the face. A downloaded font can lack one: x.com's does,
and the page crashed in the real-site A/B of the previous commit.

`family_name_or_empty` calls CTFontCopyFamilyName and treats null as "no
name". Test: Ahem with its `name` table hidden from the table directory;
it panicked ("Fonts should always have a family name") before this.
