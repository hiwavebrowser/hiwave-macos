fix(layout,text): the default font and the generic families are Chrome's faces on macOS

Unstyled text and `sans-serif` were laid out in the system font and
`monospace` in Menlo. Chrome on macOS uses Times, Helvetica and Courier.
Since #411 paint draws the face layout measured, so every page that names
no font, or only a generic, was drawn in the wrong face.

- rustkit-css: the initial `font-family` is INITIAL_FONT_FAMILY (Times on
  macOS; other platforms keep `sans-serif`), and `font-family: initial`
  gives the same value.
- rustkit-layout FontFamilyChain (macOS): `sans-serif` leads with
  Helvetica, `serif` with Times, `monospace` with Courier, `cursive` with
  Apple Chancery, `fantasy` with Papyrus; the family appended to every
  author list is the UA default, not the system font.
- rustkit-text map_generic: `serif` is Times, `monospace` is Courier.
- rustkit-text blink_ascent: Blink's macOS ascent adjustment for Times,
  Helvetica and Courier (15% of the rounded ascent + descent), so a 16px
  line in those faces is 18px as in Chrome, not 16.

Chrome 148, 28 characters at 16px: unstyled 199.52, sans-serif 217.02,
monospace 268.84, cursive 200.45, fantasy 208.02. Layout now measures
each within 0.05px.
