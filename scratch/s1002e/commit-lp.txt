fix(layout): a url() background image sits at its length position

DisplayCommand::BackgroundImage carried the position as two shares of
the free space, and layout turned a length into share 0: `10px 20px`,
every sprite offset and `right 10px` drew the image at the box's corner
(or flush on the far edge). Gradients were right, since their rect is
computed in layout.

The command now carries a pixel offset beside the share, the renderer
adds it, and `right <offset>` / `bottom <offset>` are kept by the
parser (BackgroundPositionValue::FromEnd).
