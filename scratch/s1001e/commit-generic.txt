fix(layout): `serif` and `monospace` after missing families resolve as the generic

FontFamilyChain::from_css_value expanded `sans-serif` and the system
keywords when they appeared after the first family, but pushed `serif`
and `monospace` into the chain as if they were font names. No font has
those names, so the walk went past them to the system font appended at
the end: `Consolas, monospace` was measured in a proportional face while
paint, which maps the keyword itself, drew Menlo at those advances.

Chrome 148, 28 characters at 16px: `X, monospace` 268.84, `X, serif`
199.52. Layout gave 220.78 (the system font) for both; it now gives
269.72 (Menlo) and 199.51 (Times New Roman), the same as the keyword
alone.

Test: a_generic_after_missing_families_resolves_as_the_generic fails
with the new arm disabled (left 220.78, right 269.72) and passes with it.
