fix(engine): the background shorthand sets each layer's position, size, repeat and boxes

A layer of `background` kept only its image, and only when the layer
began with it. `url(a.png) no-repeat center / cover` tiled from the top
left at its own size, `no-repeat url(a.png)` had no image, and
`linear-gradient(...) no-repeat 0 0 / 100% 2px` painted nothing (the
gradient parser was handed the whole layer).

`parse_background_shorthand_layer` reads a layer token by token
(css-backgrounds-3 §3.10): the image wherever it stands, the position
with an optional `/ size`, one or two repeat keywords, the attachment
(read, not used), one or two boxes, and the colour. A url is taken to its
closing parenthesis, not to the first `)` inside it.

`background-position` reads its keywords by axis: one value centres the
other axis and `top` / `bottom` are vertical; `top right` is `right top`;
the edge-offset form keeps an offset from `left` / `top`.
