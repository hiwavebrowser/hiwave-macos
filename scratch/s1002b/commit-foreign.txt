fix(engine): a background value in another engine's prefix is dropped whole

`background: -moz-linear-gradient(...)` (also `-o-`, `-ms-`) does not parse
in Chrome, so the declaration is ignored and the earlier background stays.
The engine cleared the earlier image layers on it, and with the shorthand
reset it would have cleared the colour too.
