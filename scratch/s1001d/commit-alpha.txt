fix(renderer): composite images source-over instead of copying them

The image batch was drawn with the blit pipeline, whose blend is REPLACE:
every texel overwrote the pixel under it, alpha included. A transparent
texel is (0, 0, 0, 0) in most PNGs, so a logo with a transparent surround
painted as a logo in a black box.

create_image_pipeline is the same shader with ALPHA_BLENDING (decoded
images are straight alpha). It shares one constructor with the colour-glyph
pipeline. Opaque images paint the same pixels as before.
