fix: box shadows and textured quads follow rounded corners

The second half of elliptical corners (#401 left both out and named them).

Shadows: DisplayCommand::BoxShadow carries the box's used border-box
radii. An outer shadow's shape is the box's shape moved and spread, with
the corner radii grown by the spread (CSS Backgrounds 3 section 6.1.1:
less for a radius smaller than the spread, never for a square corner),
and its hole is the box's own curve. rounded_difference_pieces paints a
rounded rect minus a rounded rect; two square shapes give rect_minus
exactly, so a box without border-radius gets the rects it always did.
The blur is still the stack of expanding layers; each layer's radii grow
with it. Inset shadows do not use the radii yet.

Textured quads: glyphs, colour glyphs, <img> and background tiles under a
rounded overflow clip were cut to the clip's rect only, so an avatar in a
border-radius: 50% box painted square. clip_textured_pieces_under applies
the rounded decomposition colour quads get, each piece keeping the texels
that were under it. A quad inside every rounded constraint (text between
the arcs of a pill) stays one quad.

Tests: an engine display-list pin and an engine frame test (both fail on
develop 473a047), one layout test, nine device-free renderer tests.
