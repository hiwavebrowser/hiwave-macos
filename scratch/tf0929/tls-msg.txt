perf(http): load the platform root store once per process, not per client and per h2 downgrade

The rustls client from PR 346 called rustls_native_certs::load_native_certs()
in every Client::with_config, and again inside connect_tls on every
connection whose peer selects h2. On macOS that walks the keychain trust
settings and costs seconds. Measured on develop vs 062f73a: engine start
+5.2 s, reddit's top-level GET 5.45 s vs 0.14 s, and x/wikipedia/facebook
now exceeding the 30 s capture budget. The store is now a process-wide
OnceLock, and the http/1.1-only connector for the negotiated downgrade is
built once in with_config. The TLS profile, the ALPN offer and the
downgrade are unchanged.
