fix(text): the emoji face still leads the fallback for its own characters

With the cascade first, U+23F0 and U+2328 (emoji outside is_emoji's
ranges) got a text face, and their lines lost the emoji face's height:
`about` went from 73 to 165 geometry-gate failures (a feature row 54 ->
47px, a heading 29 -> 24px; Chrome 54 and 29). The campaign's diff_pct
did not move, the gate did.

A character above Latin-1 that Apple Color Emoji has is taken from it, as
on develop. ASCII and Latin-1 are not: that face also has the digits, #,
*, (c) and (r), which are text unless a variation selector or keycap
follows, and develop drew a web font's missing digits from it.

Tests: the emoji-face pin covers U+23F0 and U+2328 and a face without
digits; the layout line-height test pins both lines at 26px.
