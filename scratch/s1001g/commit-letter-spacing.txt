fix(layout): intrinsic text widths include letter-spacing and word-spacing

The min-content and max-content width of a text run were measured
without `letter-spacing` or `word-spacing`, so a shrink-to-fit box around
spaced text was narrower than the line laid out in it. new_tab's "HIWAVE"
logo (48px, `letter-spacing: 0.5rem`, in an inline-block) was 165.66px
wide where Chrome's is 214.80, and sat 24.6px right of centre.

`text_min_content_width`, `text_max_content_width` and
`collapsed_space_width` now measure through `spaced_text_width`, which
resolves the two spacings as line layout's `shape_line` does and calls
`measure_text_with_spacing`.

Test: `a_shrink_to_fit_box_is_as_wide_as_its_spaced_text` (px and rem
letter-spacing, word-spacing). With develop's grid.rs it fails: 184.05
against 184.05 + 48.
