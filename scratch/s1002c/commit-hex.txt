fix(css): a four-digit hex colour is parsed (#rgba)

`#rgba` is the short form of `#rrggbbaa` (css-color-4): each digit is
doubled. The parser knew three, six and eight digits, so a declaration
with four was dropped and the colour under it stayed. Minifiers write
`transparent` as `#0000`: Tailwind's preflight resets every button and
field with `background-color:#0000`, and squarespace's buttons do the
same.

A hex colour is also checked to be hex digits only before it is read.

Tests: two in rustkit-css; one in the engine, which fails on develop
(`background-color: #0000` over red stays red).
