fix(layout): a fit-content height flex container is sized by its lines

Two container-side checks in layout_flex_container_at asked "is the height
anything but auto" and so counted `height: fit-content` as a definite
height: the cross size a row's lines stretch into, and the height step 12
leaves alone. The height it then used was whatever the block pre-pass had
stacked the items to. Six 32px pills on one line made a 192px container
with every pill stretched to 192.

linkedin's layered-CSS variant sizes its topic-pill rows this way
(`display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; height: fit-content`): each pill was 570
tall instead of 32.

Pin (both entry points, wrapping and not; fails first): Chrome 148 has the
pills and the container 32 tall.
