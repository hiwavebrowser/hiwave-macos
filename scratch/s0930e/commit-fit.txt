fix(layout): a fit-content height flex item keeps its content in a column

content_border_height (the vertical automatic minimum, css-flexbox-1 §4.5)
sent `height: fit-content` down the "already resolved" arm and read the
box's own height. For a `flex: 1` item that is the 0 it was squeezed to, so
the minimum was 0 and the item stayed 0 tall with its content painted over
by the next sibling. `fit-content` is content-sized, as `auto` is.

linkedin's layered-CSS variant wraps its whole page body in
`flex: 1; display: grid; height: fit-content` inside an auto-height column:
the wrapper was 0 tall around 5697px of content.

Pin (both entry points, block and grid item; fails first): Chrome 148 has
the item 80 tall and the next box at 80.
