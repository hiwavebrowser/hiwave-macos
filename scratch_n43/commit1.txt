fix(layout): a parent's open edges never collapsed margins with its children, and form controls dropped their author margins

CSS 2.1 §8.3.1 parent/child through-collapse was openly not performed:
layout_block_with_collapse gave every child list a fresh context and the
comment said so. So `.section-title{margin-bottom:10px}` followed by an
unpadded wrapper whose first child had `margin-top:4px` laid that child at
10 + 4 where Chrome puts it at max(10, 4); a wrapper-in-wrapper doubled it;
and the last child's bottom margin, when the parent's bottom edge was open,
was silently dropped instead of adjoining the parent's own bottom margin.
css-selectors carried one +4 per wrapper level and every later section rode
on it.

Now:
- Top edge: before positioning, a box adjoins its first in-flow block
  child's top margin — recursively down the chain of open first children
  (no border/padding-top, not a formatting root, no line box or float in
  between) — into the same context as its own top margin and the previous
  sibling's bottom margin. The first child takes a
  `first_child_top_adjoined` flag and contributes no top margin of its own.
- Bottom edge: when should_collapse_with_last_child() holds, the last
  child's pending margin stays in the child context
  (`last_child_collapses_through`) and is absorbed into the parent's
  context next to the parent's own bottom margin. Closed edges and
  formatting roots keep the margin inside the box (which is also new for
  flex/grid item re-layouts, where it used to be dropped).
- Formatting roots: a context flag `children_are_formatting_roots` marks
  flex/grid items (set by the container) and the root element (set by the
  engine) — their own margins never collapse through them with their
  children's (css-flexbox-1 §4; the root element's box margins do not
  collapse).

Form controls: layout_form_control never resolved margin-*, so a
`button{margin:4px}` / `input{margin:4px 0}` row measured its bare rect
(33.5 / 37.5 vs Chrome 39 / 43). Margins now resolve from style into the
margin box; the inline path already places from the margin edge.

baseline_is_bottom_edge: author-padded controls kept the bottom-edge model
because it "measured closer" — a calibration made while the margins were
missing. With margins in the margin box the hang model builds Chrome's 39
and the bottom-edge model overshoots to 41.5, so every text-line control
now uses the hang model; checkbox/radio (no inner text line) use the
bottom-edge baseline (Blink), which builds css-selectors' 23px
checkbox+label line.

Tests: rustkit-layout 308 -> 315 (first-child through an open parent;
chain through two wrappers; last-child adjoins the next sibling and does
not inflate the parent; padded parent keeps the margin inside; flex item
keeps both edge margins inside; root element does not collapse with body;
form control carries its author margins). rustkit-engine 69/69.

Board receipts in the next commit. Ledger: percent margins in the
through-chain resolve against the top box's content width; a label's text
next to a bottom-edge checkbox does not yet drop to the checkbox baseline
(Slice C vertical-align, css-selectors' last 1 geometry mismatch);
out-of-flow children are still laid out against the parent's live margin
context (pre-existing).
